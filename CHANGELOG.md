## [0.1.0] - 2025-01-01

### Added

- `SorbetConcerns::TypedConcern` — typed_class/typed_instance helpers
- `SorbetConcerns::StateTransitionValidatable` — state transition validation
- `SorbetConcerns::ImmutableFields` — immutable and write-once field validation
- `SorbetConcerns::ThreadGuardedTransition` — thread-local transition guard
